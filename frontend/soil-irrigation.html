Water-Saving Tips

Drip Irrigation
Switch to drip irrigation to save up to 40% water compared to flood irrigation. Ideal for row crops like tomatoes and cotton.
Mulching
Apply organic mulch around plants to reduce soil evaporation by 30%, suppress weeds, and maintain soil temperature.
Optimal Timing
Irrigate early morning or late evening to minimise evaporation losses. Avoid midday watering during peak sun hours.
Rainwater Harvesting
Install rainwater collection systems. A 1-acre rooftop can collect approximately 50,000 litres annually for irrigation.
Soil Moisture Monitoring
Use soil moisture sensors or the feel method to avoid over-watering. Irrigate only when soil moisture drops below 50% of field capacity.
Drought-Tolerant Crops
Choose drought-tolerant crop varieties for dry seasons. Consider crop rotation with legumes that improve soil water retention and structure.
