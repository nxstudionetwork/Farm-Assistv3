Health Tips

Monsoon Care for Paddy

Ensure proper drainage to prevent waterlogging. Apply fungicides preventively to avoid blast disease. Monitor for leaf folder pests during humid conditions.

Summer Management for Vegetables

Use mulch to retain soil moisture and reduce weed growth. Install shade nets for heat-sensitive crops. Irrigate early morning or evening to minimize evaporation.

Preventive Measures for All Crops

Rotate crops annually to break pest cycles. Maintain proper spacing for air circulation. Remove and destroy infected plant debris promptly. Use certified disease-free seeds.

Best Practices for Fertilizer Application

Conduct soil tests before applying fertilizers. Apply nitrogen in split doses for better uptake. Use organic amendments like compost to improve soil health and disease resistance.
