Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a Farm Assist account?

Download the Farm Assist app or open the web app and tap Create Account. Enter your full name, phone number, and create a Farm ID (FA-AS-XXXXXXXX). Set a 4-digit PIN for quick login. You'll receive an OTP on your phone to verify your identity. Once verified, your account is ready to use immediately.

How do I log in with my Farm ID?

Open the app and enter your Farm ID (format: FA-AS-XXXXXXXX) on the login screen. You'll then be prompted to enter your 4-digit PIN. If you've forgotten your PIN, tap Forgot PIN? and verify with the OTP sent to your registered phone number. You can also use biometric login if enabled in settings.

What is my Farm ID?

Your Farm ID is a unique identifier assigned when you register, in the format FA-AS-XXXXXXXX. It is displayed on your profile page and in the sidebar. Use it to log in, share with experts for consultations, and for farmer-to-farmer identification. Keep it safe and do not share it publicly.

How do I add my farm?

Navigate to the Farm section from the sidebar or bottom nav and tap + Add Farm. Enter your farm name, location (use the map or enter manually), total area in acres or hectares, and select the soil type. You can also draw farm boundaries on the map for satellite monitoring. Once saved, your farm will appear in the list and all features will become available for that farm.

How do I add crops and track them?

Open your farm and select a field, then tap Add Crop. Choose from the crop library (Rice, Wheat, Cotton, Maize, Vegetables, etc.), set the planting date, expected harvest date, and variety. The app automatically tracks growth stages, sends irrigation and fertilization reminders, and monitors crop health via satellite data.

How do I use AI for crop health analysis?

Go to the AI section from the bottom navigation. You can upload a photo of your crop leaf or use the camera to scan it in real-time. The AI model detects pests, diseases, and nutrient deficiencies, then provides a diagnosis with severity level and recommended treatments including both organic and chemical control options.

How do I check weather for my farm?

Open the Weather section from the bottom navigation or dashboard widget. The app automatically shows weather data for your registered farm location. View current temperature, humidity, wind speed, and a 7-day forecast. Rain alerts, heatwave warnings, and optimal spraying conditions are highlighted to help you plan farm activities.

How do I contact an expert?

Go to the Experts section from the sidebar menu. Browse the directory of verified agricultural experts by specialty (Soil Science, Crop Health, Irrigation, etc.). You can check their ratings, availability, and hourly rates. Tap Call for immediate consultation or Book to schedule a detailed session at your preferred time.

How do I use the marketplace?

Open Marketplace from the sidebar. To buy, browse categories (Seeds, Fertilizers, Pesticides, Tools), filter by price or rating, add items to your cart, and checkout. To sell, tap Sell, select your crop, set quantity, grade, and price, then list it. Buyers can contact you through the in-app chat for negotiations.

How do I book workers?

Go to Workers from the sidebar menu. Browse available workers by skill (ploughing, sowing, harvesting, spraying, etc.). Check their ratings, hourly rates, and real-time availability. Tap Book Now, select the date and time, and confirm the booking. Both you and the worker receive notifications and can track the booking status.

How do I track expenses?

Navigate to the Finance section to view your wallet and transaction history. Tap Add Expense to record costs by category (Seeds, Fertilizer, Labor, Equipment, etc.). You can also record income from sales. The app provides monthly summaries, spending breakdowns, and profit/loss reports to help you manage farm finances effectively.

How do I use offline mode?

Farm Assist works fully offline. Data is stored locally in your browser storage. You can add farms, fields, crops, expenses, and tasks without an internet connection. When you reconnect, the app automatically syncs all changes to the cloud. An offline indicator at the top of the screen shows your current connection status. Enable offline mode from Settings.

How do I get technical support?

Scroll down to the Contact Support section on this page and fill out the support form with your issue details. You can also reach the Kisan Call Centre at 1800-180-1551 (toll-free) for urgent help. For app emergencies, dial 112. Our support team typically responds within 24 hours on working days.
