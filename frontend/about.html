What Problems We Solve

1

Information Asymmetry

Farmers often lack timely access to accurate market prices, weather data, and pest alerts that large agribusinesses take for granted.

2

Crop Loss Due to Late Detection

Diseases and pest infestations destroy crops when detected too late. Our AI scans and alerts farmers at the earliest signs of trouble.

3

Unfair Market Pricing

Without transparent mandi price data, farmers are forced to accept whatever intermediaries offer. Real-time pricing restores bargaining power.

4

Wasted Water & Resources

Over-irrigation and improper fertilizer use deplete resources and harm soil. Smart scheduling optimizes every drop and every gram.

5

Missed Government Benefits

Thousands of eligible farmers never apply for subsidies and schemes due to complex paperwork and lack of awareness.

6

Isolation & Limited Knowledge Sharing

Farmers in remote areas have limited access to agronomy experts and peer learning. Our community connects them to a world of shared wisdom.
