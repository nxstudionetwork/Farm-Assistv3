Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact support?

You can reach our support team through the Help & Support page, by emailing farm.assist@outlook.com, or by calling our Kisan Call Centre at 1800-180-1551 (Toll Free). Our team is available Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 8 PM IST.

Where can I find tutorials and guides?

Head over to the Help & Support page. We offer step-by-step guides covering everything from setting up your farm profile to managing crops and using the marketplace.

How do I report a bug?

Use the feedback form above and select "Bug Report" as the type. Include as much detail as possible: what you were doing, what you expected, and what actually happened. Our developers review every report within 48 hours.

What happens after I submit feedback?

Your feedback is sent directly to the Farm Assist team via email and stored securely in our database. We review every submission and use your input to prioritize improvements and fixes. Bug reports are typically addressed within 48 hours.
