Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Farm Assist collects information you provide directly, including:

  • Full name, phone number, email address
  • Farmer ID and authentication credentials
  • Farm location, size, and crop data
  • Profile photos and document uploads
  • Device location (with your consent)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide personalized farming recommendations
  • Enable AI-powered crop disease diagnosis
  • Show weather forecasts for your location
  • Connect you with workers, experts, and buyers
  • Process marketplace orders and payments
  • Send relevant government scheme notifications

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service providers (AI, weather, maps APIs) under strict data processing agreements
  • Government authorities when required by law
  • Other farmers only when you explicitly choose to share (community features)

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time. Analytics data is retained for 24 months.

5. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data in machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
