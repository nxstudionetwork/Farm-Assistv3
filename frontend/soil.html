Soil Amendments

Lime Application: Raises pH in acidic soils. Apply 2t/ha for clay soils.
Gypsum: Improves sodic soil structure. Apply 5t/ha for reclamation.
Organic Matter: Add 5t/ha compost yearly to maintain soil health.
Green Manure: Grow sunn hemp or dhaincha and plough back for nitrogen.
Bio-fertilizers: Rhizobium, Azotobacter, PSB reduce chemical fertilizer need.
