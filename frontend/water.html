Water & Irrigation

Manage water sources, schedules, and conservation

Water Saving Tips

Drip Irrigation: Saves up to 40% water compared to flood irrigation. Best for row crops.
Mulching: Reduce evaporation by 30% using organic mulch around plants.
Rainwater Harvesting: 1 acre rooftop can collect ~50,000L annually.
Irrigation Timing: Water early morning or evening to reduce evaporation loss.
Soil Moisture Sensors: Smart sensors prevent over-watering and save 20% water.
Deficit Irrigation: Apply water only during critical growth stages for 15% savings.
